A traditional VPN puts a remote device on your network, which means a compromised laptop is a compromised network. Zero-trust access grants only specific applications. If you’re building remote access from scratch in 2026, start with the zero-trust model Tailscale, Harmony SASE, or the ZTNA features inside Fortinet and Palo Alto rather than defaulting to the VPN you’ve always run. If you already own the firewall, start there. Fortinet, Palo Alto, Check Point, and Cisco all include remote access with infrastructure most organizations already have. Buying a separate VPN platform when your firewall does the job is a common and avoidable expense. Size for peak, not average. Remote access capacity planning fails at the worst moments a snow day, an office closure, an incident. Ask about concurrent connection limits on your specific hardware or tier, and what happens when you exceed them. Treat the VPN as internet-facing attack surface, because it is. Remote access gateways have been among the most heavily exploited enterprise components in recent years when managing internet-exposed attack surfaces, with edge devices repeatedly appearing in national cyber agency advisories. Whatever you choose, commit to patching within days of vendor release, enforce MFA without exception, and monitor authentication logs. Common mistakes: enabling split tunnelling without understanding what bypasses inspection; leaving decommissioned staff accounts active on the VPN; and running self-hosted access servers that nobody has patched in a year. Situational FAQ What is a business VPN? A business VPN creates an encrypted tunnel between remote users or sites and the corporate network so traffic crossing the public internet cannot be read or altered. Traditional VPNs grant network-level access once connected, while newer zero-trust alternatives grant access only to specific applications. Which business VPN is best for a small business? Tailscale is the fastest to deploy for technical teams and NordLayer is the easiest for non-technical ones, both with published pricing. If you already run a FortiGate or similar firewall, its built-in remote access is usually the better value since you have already paid for it. Is a VPN still necessary in 2026? For legacy applications and site-to-site connectivity, yes. For general remote worker access, zero-trust network access is increasingly the better model because it grants application-level rather than network-level access, limiting what an attacker can reach if a device or credential is compromised. Most organizations run both during a multi-year transition. What is the difference between a business VPN and ZTNA? A VPN places the device on the corporate network, so anything reachable from that network is reachable by the device. ZTNA authenticates the user and device for each application individually and never exposes the network itself. ZTNA also applies continuous verification rather than trusting the session after initial connection. How much do business VPN solutions cost? Cloud VPN services with published pricing typically run a modest per-user monthly fee with tiers for advanced features. Enterprise platforms from Cisco, Palo Alto, Fortinet, and Check Point are quote-based and bundled with firewall licensing. OpenVPN Access Server is the least expensive commercial option, with the trade-off that you operate it yourself. Can I use a consumer VPN for business? No. Consumer VPN services are built to mask your traffic from your internet provider, not to connect you to corporate resources. They lack centralized management, directory integration, device posture checks, audit logging, and per-user access control all of which business remote access requires. The Short Version Tailscale is the best starting point for small technical teams, NordLayer for small businesses that want simple and managed, and Fortinet, Cisco, or Palo Alto if you already own the firewall and simply need to turn remote access on. Absolute (NetMotion) is in a category of its own for mobile field workforces, and Check Point Harmony SASE is the cleanest path if you know you’re heading toward full SASE. Before you buy anything, ask whether you actually need network access or just application access the answer usually points away from a traditional VPN.
